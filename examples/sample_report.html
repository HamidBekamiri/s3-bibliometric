Q4

Does S3 agree with classic bibliometric methods?

Recommended
Validates S3 against bibliographic coupling and co-citation. · Method: Pearson correlation, top-10 neighbour overlap, NMI/ARI vs BC communities
  • S3 correlates r = 0.68 with bibliographic coupling and r = 0.14 with co-citation.
  • On average 14% of a paper's 10 nearest neighbours are shared between S3 and bibliographic coupling (Jaccard).
  • Communities from bibliographic coupling agree with S3 communities at NMI = 1.00, ARI = 1.00.
  • 0 of 151 papers have no references and can only be mapped by S3 (an advantage of the semantic approach).
